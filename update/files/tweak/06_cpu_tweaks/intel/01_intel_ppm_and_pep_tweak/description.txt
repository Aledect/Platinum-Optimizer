This implementation is designed for Intel architectures and acts synergistically on the intelppm.sys (Processor Device Driver) and intelpep.sys (Power Engine Plug-in) drivers without compromising their base execution state. By keeping the service active (Start=3), the processor retains full support for dynamic hardware technologies (Intel Turbo Boost, Speed Shift / Hardware Controlled P-States, and deep C-State transitions), preventing the frequencies from being locked to the base clock.
